Examples

Models: many icons

runtime.model(json) parses an icon once; model.icon() draws from it with a playback and look of its own, at almost no cost in time or memory (50 to 470 KB less for each icon after the first). Untick “one model per file” to load every icon on its own, as runtime.load() does. Every icon loops; one clock ticks them all and draws on the main thread.
